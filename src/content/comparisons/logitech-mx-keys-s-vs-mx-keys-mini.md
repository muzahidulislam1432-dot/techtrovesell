# Logitech MX Keys S vs Logitech MX Keys Mini (2026): Which Keyboard Should You Buy?

## Introduction

A great keyboard can significantly improve your daily productivity, whether you're writing emails, coding, editing documents, designing graphics, or managing spreadsheets. While many wireless keyboards offer similar features, choosing the right layout and typing experience can have a noticeable impact on comfort and efficiency.

Two of Logitech's most popular premium productivity keyboards are the **MX Keys S** and the **MX Keys Mini**. Both belong to Logitech's flagship MX lineup and share many core features, including smart backlighting, multi-device connectivity, USB-C charging, and compatibility with Logi Options+. However, they are designed for different users and work environments.

The **Logitech MX Keys S** is a full-size wireless keyboard that includes a dedicated numeric keypad, making it an excellent choice for office professionals, accountants, programmers, and anyone who spends long hours typing. The **Logitech MX Keys Mini**, on the other hand, offers the same premium typing experience in a compact layout that saves desk space and is easier to carry while traveling. Logitech positions both keyboards as premium productivity tools with quiet, laptop-style keys and multi-device support.

In this detailed comparison, we'll examine their design, typing comfort, layout, battery life, connectivity, software, portability, and overall value to help you decide which keyboard best matches your workflow.

***

# Quick Verdict

If you mainly work from a desk and frequently use spreadsheets, accounting software, or any application that benefits from a numeric keypad, the **Logitech MX Keys S** is the better overall choice. Its full-size layout, comfortable key spacing, and dedicated number pad make it ideal for long productivity sessions.

If portability and desk space are more important, the **Logitech MX Keys Mini** is the stronger option. It offers nearly the same premium typing experience while taking up much less room, making it perfect for hybrid workers, students, and users who frequently switch between locations.

# Design & Build Quality

## Logitech MX Keys S

The MX Keys S follows a traditional full-size keyboard layout with a dedicated numeric keypad, full navigation cluster, and function row. Its solid aluminum top plate gives the keyboard a premium appearance while adding rigidity that helps prevent flex during typing.

The larger footprint provides generous spacing between keys, making it comfortable for long work sessions. Users who regularly enter numbers or work with financial data will appreciate the integrated numpad, which can improve efficiency compared to compact layouts.

Despite its larger size, the keyboard maintains a clean and modern design that fits well in professional office setups. Logitech also uses low-profile keys with slightly concave keycaps that naturally guide your fingertips into position, helping reduce typing errors over time.

### Pros

- Premium aluminum construction
- Full-size layout with numeric keypad
- Excellent stability on the desk
- Spacious key arrangement
- Professional appearance

***

## Logitech MX Keys Mini

The MX Keys Mini shares the same premium materials and overall design language but removes the numeric keypad and several dedicated navigation keys to create a much smaller footprint.

This compact layout frees up valuable desk space and allows the mouse to sit closer to the keyboard, which many users find more comfortable for long work sessions. It is also easier to pack into backpacks and laptop bags, making it a strong choice for professionals who travel or frequently work from different locations.

Although it is smaller, the typing quality remains almost identical to the MX Keys S because Logitech uses the same key switches and keycap design across both models.

### Pros

- Compact and lightweight
- Premium build quality
- Saves desk space
- Easy to transport
- Ideal for minimalist workstations

## Smart Backlighting

One of the standout features shared by both the **Logitech MX Keys S** and **MX Keys Mini** is Logitech's intelligent backlighting system.

Unlike traditional backlit keyboards that remain illuminated continuously, these keyboards use built-in proximity sensors to detect when your hands approach. The keys automatically light up before you begin typing and adjust brightness based on the ambient lighting in your room. This improves visibility while helping conserve battery life.

### Benefits

- Automatic hand detection
- Ambient light adjustment
- Manual brightness control through Logi Options+
- Improved battery efficiency
- Comfortable typing in low-light environments

The illumination is subtle rather than overly bright, making it ideal for offices, home workspaces, and late-night productivity.
