# Logitech MX Master 3S vs Logitech MX Anywhere 3S (2026): Which Mouse Should You Buy?

## Introduction

Choosing the right productivity mouse can make a noticeable difference in your daily workflow. Whether you're editing documents, working with spreadsheets, creating content, programming, or simply browsing the web, a comfortable and responsive mouse helps reduce fatigue while improving efficiency.

Two of Logitech's most popular premium wireless mice are the **MX Master 3S** and the **MX Anywhere 3S**. At first glance they share many of the same technologies, including an 8,000 DPI Darkfield sensor, quiet clicks, USB-C charging, multi-device connectivity, and compatibility with Logi Options+. However, they are designed for different types of users.

The **MX Master 3S** is a full-size ergonomic mouse built for professionals who spend long hours at a desk. It includes additional controls such as a horizontal scroll wheel and a thumb-rest gesture button, making it especially useful for productivity applications. The **MX Anywhere 3S**, on the other hand, focuses on portability with a smaller, lighter design that easily fits into a laptop bag while retaining many of Logitech's flagship features.

In this detailed comparison, we'll examine their design, comfort, features, performance, battery life, software, and overall value to help you decide which mouse best suits your workflow.

***

# Quick Verdict

If you primarily work from a desk and spend several hours each day using a mouse, the **Logitech MX Master 3S** is the stronger overall choice. Its ergonomic shape, secondary scroll wheel, additional programmable controls, and premium feel make it one of the best productivity mice available.

If portability is your top priority, the **Logitech MX Anywhere 3S** is the better option. Its compact size and lighter weight make it ideal for travel, hybrid work, and users who frequently switch between locations without giving up premium features like Darkfield tracking or multi-device pairing.

### Our Recommendation

🏆 **Best Overall Productivity Mouse:** Logitech MX Master 3S

🎒 **Best Portable Productivity Mouse:** Logitech MX Anywhere 3S

# Design & Build Quality

## Logitech MX Master 3S

The MX Master 3S features a premium ergonomic design that is specifically shaped for right-handed users. Its sculpted body naturally supports the palm, thumb, and fingers, reducing wrist movement during extended work sessions.

The mouse is constructed from high-quality materials with a soft-touch finish that feels durable and comfortable. Logitech has also positioned the thumb buttons, gesture button, and horizontal scroll wheel within easy reach, making multitasking significantly more efficient.

Although the MX Master 3S is noticeably larger and heavier than the MX Anywhere 3S, that extra size contributes to its excellent stability and comfort for prolonged desktop use.

### Pros

- Premium build quality
- Excellent ergonomics
- Comfortable for long working sessions
- Additional horizontal scroll wheel
- Easy-to-reach controls

***

## Logitech MX Anywhere 3S

The MX Anywhere 3S follows a different design philosophy. Instead of maximizing comfort for desk work, it prioritizes portability.

Its compact body easily fits into backpacks, laptop sleeves, or even jacket pockets, making it an excellent companion for professionals who frequently travel or work in different locations.

Despite its smaller footprint, the build quality remains excellent. The mouse still offers premium materials, precise construction, quiet buttons, and Logitech's MagSpeed scroll wheel.

Because of its symmetrical shape, it also accommodates a wider variety of grip styles, although users with larger hands may find it less comfortable during long work sessions than the MX Master 3S.

### Pros

- Compact and lightweight
- Easy to carry
- Premium construction
- Excellent travel companion
- Comfortable for smaller hands

# Comfort & Ergonomics

Comfort is one of the biggest differences between these two mice.

If you spend eight or more hours each day working at a desk, the MX Master 3S provides a more natural hand position thanks to its sculpted ergonomic shape. The thumb rest helps reduce strain, while the larger body allows your entire hand to rest comfortably without excessive finger tension.

The MX Anywhere 3S, while still comfortable for everyday use, requires a more compact grip. For users with smaller hands or those who primarily use a laptop on the go, this design can actually feel more natural. However, larger-handed users may experience more fatigue during extended sessions because the palm isn't supported to the same extent. Community discussions also commonly highlight the tradeoff between the Master 3S's superior ergonomics and the Anywhere 3S's lighter, travel-friendly form factor.

### Ergonomics Winner

🏆 **Logitech MX Master 3S**

Its full-size ergonomic design is simply better suited to long productivity sessions, making it the stronger choice for office professionals, developers, designers, and anyone who spends most of the day at a desk.
