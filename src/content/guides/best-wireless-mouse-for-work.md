# How to Choose the Right Wireless Mouse

Before buying, consider:

### Ergonomics

Choose a shape that fits your hand size.

### Battery

Rechargeable mice reduce waste, while AA-powered models often last many months.

### Connectivity

Bluetooth is convenient, while USB receivers can offer lower latency.

### Buttons

Extra programmable buttons can improve productivity.

### Sensor Accuracy

Higher DPI isn't always necessary, but smooth tracking is important.

***

# Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Bluetooth or a USB receiver better?

Both work well. Bluetooth reduces USB port usage, while dedicated receivers may offer a more stable connection in busy wireless environments.

***

### Are expensive mice worth it?

Premium mice often provide better ergonomics, build quality, and productivity features. If you spend many hours at a computer, the investment can be worthwhile.

***

### Can one mouse control multiple computers?

Many modern productivity mice support switching between multiple paired devices with a dedicated button or software.
