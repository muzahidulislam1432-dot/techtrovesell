## Choose the right size

Full-size, TKL, 75%, 65% and 60% layouts trade dedicated keys for desk space.

## Understand switch feel

Linear, tactile and clicky switches create different typing and gaming experiences.

## Check connectivity

Compare wired, Bluetooth and low-latency wireless options based on your devices.

## Look beyond lighting

Stabilisers, keycaps, software and build quality often matter more than RGB.
