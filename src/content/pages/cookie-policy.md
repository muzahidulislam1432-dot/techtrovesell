### What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device to help websites function efficiently.

### Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

- Improve website performance.
- Remember preferences.
- Analyze traffic.
- Measure content performance.
- Support affiliate tracking.

### Types of Cookies

**Essential Cookies**

Required for website functionality.

**Performance Cookies**

Help us understand how visitors interact with the site.

**Analytics Cookies**

Used by services such as Google Analytics.

**Affiliate Cookies**

Some affiliate partners use cookies to properly attribute purchases.

### Managing Cookies

You can disable cookies anytime through your browser settings.

Please note that disabling cookies may affect certain website features.
