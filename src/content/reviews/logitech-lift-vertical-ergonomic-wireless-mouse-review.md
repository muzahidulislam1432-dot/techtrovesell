# Introduction

If you spend long hours working at a computer, choosing the right mouse can make a noticeable difference in your comfort and productivity. Traditional mice often require your wrist to remain flat against the desk, which may lead to fatigue or discomfort during extended use. The Logitech Lift Vertical Ergonomic Wireless Mouse is designed to solve this problem with a 57-degree vertical grip that promotes a more natural hand position while maintaining excellent everyday performance.

Unlike gaming mice or feature-packed productivity models such as the Logitech MX Master 3S, the Lift focuses on ergonomic comfort. It combines quiet clicks, SmartWheel scrolling, customizable buttons, Bluetooth and Logi Bolt connectivity, and impressive battery life, making it an excellent choice for office professionals, remote workers, and students.

In this review, we'll examine its design, comfort, performance, battery life, software, and overall value to help you decide whether the Logitech Lift is the right ergonomic mouse for your needs.
