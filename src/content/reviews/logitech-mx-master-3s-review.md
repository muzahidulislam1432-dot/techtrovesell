# Design & Build Quality

The first thing you'll notice is the premium construction.

The MX Master 3S features a sculpted ergonomic design that naturally supports your palm, fingers, and thumb. Instead of forcing your hand into a flat position, it encourages a relaxed grip that helps reduce fatigue during extended work sessions.

The soft-touch finish gives the mouse a premium feel without being slippery, while the build quality is excellent with no noticeable flex or creaking.

For users with medium to large hands, the MX Master 3S feels exceptionally comfortable.

# Performance

The MX Master 3S uses Logitech's Darkfield sensor, allowing it to track accurately on a wide range of surfaces—including glass under certain conditions.

Cursor movement feels smooth and precise, making everyday tasks effortless.

The adjustable sensitivity allows users to customize pointer speed for different workflows, whether they need fast navigation across multiple monitors or slower, precise movements for detailed editing.

Although it isn't designed as a competitive gaming mouse, its tracking performance is excellent for productivity applications.

# MagSpeed Scroll Wheel

One of the standout features is the MagSpeed electromagnetic scroll wheel.

Unlike traditional mechanical wheels, it automatically switches between:

- Precision scrolling
- High-speed scrolling

This makes navigating long documents, spreadsheets, PDFs, and web pages incredibly fast while still allowing precise line-by-line control when needed.

Once you become accustomed to this feature, it's difficult to return to a standard scroll wheel.
